Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information SimpleDiscordBot ("the bot", "we") handles, why, and what rights you have. It is written to meet the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

In short: the bot does not keep any information about individual users. To produce a member-list export it reads usernames from Discord and hands them straight to the server manager who asked for them, without saving a copy. The only things it stores are the schedule settings a server sets up (which channel to clear, and when) and, while a channel is being cleared, how far it has got.

The bot is operated by Laurie Brown, based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the processing described here. You can get in touch at [email address hidden from spam bots; please enable JavaScript to see it].

What the bot handles

The bot fetches the member list from Discord and builds a CSV file of usernames for that person. No. The file is built in memory, sent privately to the person who asked for it, then discarded. Server ID, channel ID and schedule settings (time, day, whether to keep pinned messages) When a server manager sets up a scheduled purge with /schedule add, so the bot knows what to clear and when. Yes, until the schedule is removed. It is also deleted automatically within an hour of the bot being removed from the server, or once the bot finds the channel has been deleted. This is not information about any individual.
Server ID, channel ID and progress of a purge in progress Large channels are cleared in chunks over several minutes, so the bot records how far it has got. Only until that purge finishes, then deleted.
Basic technical logs The bot logs errors and which channels were cleared (by channel and server ID) so problems can be fixed. Kept for a few days by our hosting provider, then deleted automatically. Logs do not include usernames or message content.

The bot does not track activity, build profiles, use cookies, or sell or share data with anyone.

Our lawful basis

We rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR): server managers have a legitimate interest in moderating their own servers and knowing who their members are. The processing is limited to what is needed, the usernames are already visible to every member of the server, and nothing is retained, so we consider the impact on members to be minimal.

Member-list exports

Once a CSV file has been sent to a server manager, that copy is theirs. The server manager who downloads it is responsible for how they use, store and share it, and for doing so lawfully. We have no access to downloaded files.

Who else is involved

  • Discord, which provides the platform and holds all of your account and message data. Discord's handling of your data is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the bot (Cloudflare Workers) and stores the schedule settings and logs described above, in a database located in Western Europe. Requests to the bot, including member lists while an export is being built, pass through Cloudflare's network, which may process them outside the UK. Cloudflare acts as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum, which includes safeguards for international transfers; see also Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct or erase personal data held about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because the bot does not store information about individual users, there will normally be nothing for us to provide or delete, but you are welcome to contact us at [email address hidden from spam bots; please enable JavaScript to see it] and we will respond within one month.

If you would rather not be included in a server's member list, the simplest option is to speak to that server's managers, or leave the server.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or older where local law requires). The bot is intended for use within those limits.
