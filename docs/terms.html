What the bot does

The bot can delete all messages in a channel, either when a server manager asks or on a schedule they set, can repost a channel's messages under the bot's name and delete the originals ("anonymise"), and can produce a CSV file of the server's members' usernames, display names and roles for server managers. How it handles data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Deleting messages is permanent

Messages deleted by the bot cannot be recovered by us or by Discord. Server managers are responsible for checking that a channel should be cleared before running a purge or setting up a schedule, and for making sure any messages they need are saved elsewhere first.

Anonymising channels

Anonymising a channel permanently deletes the original messages and replaces them with copies posted by the bot. This includes the channel's threads: threads started from a message are recreated under the copy of that message (so links to them change) and the old thread is deleted, and other threads are anonymised where they are. Original timestamps, reactions and reply links are lost, and some attachments may be too large to copy. It removes names, not identities, so it is not a guarantee of anonymity. Don't use it to misrepresent what someone said, to hide wrongdoing, or to repost content you have no right to share.

Don't use exported lists for spam, unsolicited messages, or sharing members' details outside the server without good reason.
